Fashion Design — Enrollment Open

2½ years · Two stages · Tezira House, Kampala

Offered on a 95% practical basis for 2½ years — an intense, hands-on system that instils up-to-date entrepreneurial skills and attitude to meet the ever-evolving design market demands.

01Stage One — 1½ years

One and a half years through all the basics and specialised professional techniques of fashion craftsmanship.

02Stage Two — 1 Year — Creation

An introduction to creation, innovation and the management of at least four fashion projects, plus one graduation fashion-collection project — each executed independently.
