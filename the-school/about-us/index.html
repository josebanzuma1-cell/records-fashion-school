Who we are

Records Fashion School is a community-based vocational institute, established on 7 July 2008 — nurturing passionate people into professional fashion designers with an artistic mind-set that meets the evolving design market demands.

From our first graduation in 2010 to date, we have passed out 921 fashion professionals — and 91% of them have gone straight on to start design houses of their own. Our intense, hands-on system builds each student a reasonable clientele base a full year before releasing them into the world of work.

01Our Mission

To nurture and develop passionate people into professional fashion designers, with an artistic mind-set that meets the evolving design market demands.

02Our Vision

To become a central hub in research and innovations in both traditional and contemporary forms of garment design and textiles, in Africa and beyond.

03Our Promise

To impart entrepreneurial skills and attitude to enhance the productivity and competitiveness of the youth in the garment design and textile industry.

04Our Positioning

We re-imagine the world based on artistry and mastery as essential tools for success in quality design to shape the future.

Our values

Attention to Detail

A great designer is made from the time and care they put into their work. We teach one-on-one, training our students to always see the unseen — and cater for its needs in their work.

Artistry

We express this through creativity, drawing inspiration from the artistic perspective of the environments around us — guiding our students through art as a core of the design work they do.

Self-Esteem

Records Fashion School is home to garment design — a DNA-coded art, in a team of instructors who instinctively train, guide and develop talent into professional fashion designers able to challenge and meet the ever-evolving design market demands.
